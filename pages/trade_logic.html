Step 1 — Day Quality Grade (SPY only)

A 0–8 score across four factors grades how tradeable the whole session looks. _grade_day_quality()

FactorScoreRule
Gap + Overnight range0–2 has_gap = gap ≥ 0.5× median 20-day gap · has_pm_range = premarket (08:00–09:30) range ≥ 0.7× its 20-day average. Both → 2, either → 1, neither → 0.
Structure0–2 Trending → 2 · Ranging → 1 · Choppy → 0 (from Step 2 regime).
Intraday range trend (ADR)0–2 ratio = ADR(8d) / ADR(20d), gap-excluded. >1.1 → 2 · ≥0.9 → 1 · else 0. Isolates intraday follow-through from the overnight gap.
Index alignment0–2 Precomputed — do the tracked symbols agree on direction intraday. _compute_alignment_score()

Grade: total ≥7 → A+ · ≥5 → A · ≥3 → B · else C. Weekends → N/A.

Step 2 — Market Regime (from SPY)

Regime gates which patterns can fire (e.g. Gap Continuation and ORB-watch require Trending). _detect_regime()

FieldRule
label / directionClose > MA20 and MA20 rising (vs 10 bars ago) → Trending up. Close < MA20 and MA20 falling → Trending down. Otherwise Ranging.
atr_trendATR(14) vs its 20-day average: >1.1× → expanding · <0.9× → contracting · else normal.
day_typeLast completed daily bar vs prior: contained → inside (coil) · engulfs both sides → outside (expansion) · else normal.
index_alignmentHourly % moves of all symbols agree in sign (>±0.5%) → aligned, else diverging.
Choppy overrideRanging and contracting and not an outside day → relabel Choppy.

Volatility regime _classify_vol_regime()

ATR(14) percentile over trailing 1 year (252d): >85 → Extreme · >60 → Elevated · ≥25 → Normal · else Low.

Step 3 — Pattern Scanner (per symbol)

Gap: Continuation vs Fill size-gated today

Significance is relative to each symbol's own typical overnight gap — not a fixed %. lines 740–862

TermDefinition
gap_pts|today_open − prev_close| in dollars.
median gapMedian of |open − prev_close| over the last 20 days for that symbol.
gap_significantgap_pts ≥ 0.5× median → qualifies as Gap Fill.
gap_stronggap_pts ≥ 1.5× median → qualifies as Gap Continuation.
ContinuationRequires gap_strong AND regime = Trending. Direction = gap direction. Targets = open ± 1.5× / 2.0× ATR.
FillSignificant but not strong (or not Trending). Direction = toward prior close (opposite the gap). Target = prev_close.

Worked example (SPY, 2026-07-13): gap 2.48 pts = 1.1× median → significant (≥0.5) but not strong (<1.5), so Gap Fill, even in a Trending regime. USO gapped 1.8× median → strong → Gap Continuation.

Known Limitations & Roadmap

This section is the living improvement log — where the model is intentionally simple today and what we plan to refine. Update as changes land.

GapStatus & plan
Overnight range ignored in classification Gap classification uses only open − prev_close (a single point). The full overnight/weekend range — e.g. a wide range that round-trips back to flat before the open — is captured in the intraday files but is not fed into pattern classification. Only the 08:00–09:30 premarket range enters Day Quality. Plan: fold overnight range in so a small gap after a wide, reverted overnight session (a failed-move / exhaustion tell) reads differently from a genuinely quiet gap.
Size gates continuation, not trend Continuation is gated by gap size (≥1.5× median) rather than by whether the gap agrees with the trend. A small gap with the trend can be a better continuation than a large gap against it. Plan: make trend-alignment a first-class continuation qualifier; demote raw size to a confidence modifier.
Vol expansion / compression underused ATR trend and squeeze are computed but don't yet weight pattern quality. ORB breakouts tend to work when range is expanding and fail when compressing. Plan: favor ORB in expansion, penalize in compression; treat ADR(8d)/ADR(20d) and squeeze status as pattern-probability inputs.
Single label, no probability or invalidation Each setup emits one label. Plan: emit a ranked list of valid patterns, each with a probability and an explicit invalidation level, while the page keeps leading with the highest-probability one.
