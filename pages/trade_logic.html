Trade Signal Logic

How the Trade page decides what it shows you: a morning setup built before the open, and an end-of-day scorecard of what the session actually delivered.

Reading the Page

The page is written twice a day — once before the open and once after the close — and it always tells you which version you are looking at. That label matters, because the two halves of the page are allowed to see different things.

LabelWhat it meansWhat is shown
Pre-Open Today's session hasn't opened. Built from completed prior sessions plus this morning's premarket bars. Morning Setup only. End of Day is withheld.
In Progress The session is open and still moving. Morning Setup only. Intraday values would change before the close, so no result is published.
Complete The session has closed and its daily bar is final. Morning Setup and End of Day outcomes.

The rule behind this: nothing scored before the close is permitted to look at the session it describes. Day quality, regime and day structure read only bars from sessions that have already finished. Conversely, everything on the End of Day tab describes the session itself, so it stays empty until that session's bar is final — a half-finished day is never presented as a final one.

Morning Setup before the open

Six steps, narrowing from "is today worth trading at all" down to "how many shares." Each step can lower conviction; only the day-quality gate can shut the day off entirely.

Step 1 — Day Quality (scored on SPY, applies to the whole day)

Four factors, 0–2 each, for a 0–8 forecast of how tradeable the session looks.

FactorWhat it asksScoring
Gap + premarket range Did anything happen overnight? A gap moves the day's starting point; a live premarket range says participants are already engaged. Gap counts when it is ≥ 0.5× the symbol's median 20-day gap. Premarket range counts when the 08:00–09:30 ET range is ≥ 0.7× its 20-session median. Both → 2, either → 1, neither → 0.
Structure Is there a trend to trade with? Trending → 2 · Ranging → 1 · Choppy → 0. Taken from the Step 2 regime.
Intraday range trend Is the intraday tape expanding or drying up? Measured gap-excluded, so an overnight gap can't disguise a dead session. ratio = average daily range over 8 sessions ÷ over 20 sessions. >1.1 → 2 · ≥0.9 → 1 · else 0.
Index alignment Do SPY, QQQ and IWM agree on direction? All non-flat indices agree → 2 · a majority of two agree → 1 · split → 0. Moves within ±0.5% count as flat; if all three are flat, scores a neutral 1.

Grade: 7–8 → A+ Strong · 5–6 → A Favorable · 3–4 → B Selective · 0–2 → C Sit Out. A grade of C stops the page here — Steps 2–6 are not shown, because the day did not clear the gate. Before the open, index alignment has no session to read yet and contributes its neutral 1.

Step 2 — Market Regime (derived from SPY)

The regime describes the backdrop. It shapes which patterns are favoured and which gap setups can fire at all — but with one exception below, it does not by itself remove a setup from the page.

FieldHow it's decided
Trending upSPY closed above its 20-day MA and that MA is higher than it was 10 sessions ago.
Trending downClosed below the 20-day MA and the MA is falling.
RangingAnything else — price and its average disagree, or the average is flat.
ChoppyRanging and range is contracting and the last bar was not an outside day and there is no live evidence of expansion. All four must hold.
ATR trendATR(14) against its own 20-session average: >1.1× expanding · <0.9× contracting · otherwise normal.
Day structureLast completed bar vs the one before it — fully contained → inside (coiling) · engulfing both sides → outside (expansion) · otherwise normal.
Volatility regimeWhere ATR(14) sits in its own trailing one-year range: >85th → Extreme · >60th → Elevated · 25th–60th → Normal · below → Low.

Why "evidence of expansion" is part of the Choppy test: ATR(14) is a two-week average, so it still reads "contracting" on a day that doubles its recent range. An outside bar, a wide premarket, an outsized gap, or a realized range of at least 1× ATR all disqualify the chop label.

RegimePatterns it favours
TrendingORB, Gap Fill, Gap Continuation, Engulfing with the trend
RangingGap Fill, Gap Continuation, Outside Day, Engulfing at support/resistance
ChoppyGap Fill, Outside Day, Engulfing — mean reversion: fade, don't chase

A setup outside its regime's list is still shown; it simply loses one confluence point in Step 4. Regime is a preference, not a veto.

Step 3 — Pattern Scanner (per symbol)

Gap significance is measured against each symbol's own typical overnight gap, not a fixed percentage — a 2-point gap is routine in SPY and enormous in USO.

PatternFires whenPlan
Gap Fill Gap is at least 0.5× the symbol's median 20-day gap. Also the fallback when a large gap occurs outside a Trending regime. Trade back toward the prior close — direction is against the gap. Target is the prior close.
Gap Continuation Gap is at least 1.5× the median and the regime is Trending. Both required. Trade with the gap. Targets are the open ± 1.5× and 2.0× ATR.
ORB (opening range breakout) The 09:30–10:30 ET range is wider than 0.75× the 20-session average ATR — wide enough to be worth breaking. Break of the opening range high or low. Targets ± 1.5× and 2.0× ATR from that edge.
ORB watch The opening range is too narrow to qualify, but the regime is Trending and the premarket range is active. Flagged to watch. No levels are published — there is no range to break yet.
Outside Day Today's bar engulfs the prior bar on both sides and closes in the top 25% (up) or bottom 25% (down) of its range. Next session. Entry beyond today's extreme, stop at the opposite extreme, target 1.5× today's range.
Engulfing A bullish or bearish engulfing bar on above-average volume. Volume confirmation is required. Next session. Entry beyond today's extreme, stop at the opposite extreme, targets 1.5× and 2.0× ATR.

Context, not entries: the squeeze reading (hourly Bollinger width against Keltner channels, plus the sign of momentum), position against session VWAP, and hourly RSI divergence are all displayed for context. None of them opens a trade on its own.

Step 4 — Confluence Scoring

Every detected setup is checked against eight independent conditions. The count is the conviction score, and conviction is what sets position size.

CheckPasses when
Volume above 20-day averageThe daily bar traded more than its 20-session average volume.
Premarket range activePremarket range reached at least 0.7× its 20-session median.
RSI extremeDaily RSI(14) is below 35 or above 65 — not sitting in the dead zone.
MACD alignedThe daily MACD histogram points the same way as the setup.
20-day MA alignedPrice is on the setup's side of its 20-day moving average.
Day graded A or A+The Step 1 forecast cleared "Favorable".
Regime matchesThe pattern appears in the current regime's favoured list.
Squeeze alignedA squeeze is active and its momentum is building in the setup's direction.

Setups scoring below 3 of 8 are dropped. 6–8 → full size · 4–5 → 75% size · 3 → 50% size. Cards are also tagged Tue–Thu or Mon/Fri, a day-of-week note carried for observation — it does not change the score or the size.

Step 5 — Trade Levels

Each surviving setup is written out as a concrete plan, sized off ATR(14).

ElementRule
EntryPattern-specific: the gap direction, an opening-range edge, or yesterday's extreme for next-session setups.
StopThe structural invalidation level — the opposite side of the opening range, the other extreme of the signal bar, or 1× ATR from entry where no structure applies.
T1 / T2 / T3Scaled out in equal thirds: 1.5× ATR, 2.0× ATR, then a 1× ATR trailing stop on the remainder. Gap Fill uses the prior close as T1 instead, since that is the whole thesis.

Step 6 — Position Size

Risk-first sizing: decide what you're willing to lose, then let the stop distance determine the share count.

InputValue
Risk per trade1% of the account size you enter.
Stop distance1.5× ATR(14).
Shares(risk ÷ stop distance) × the size modifier from the Step 4 score.

End of Day after the close

The morning is a forecast. This tab is the scorecard — what the session actually delivered, and whether each published level was reached. It appears only once the session's daily bar is final.

What actually happened (0–8, same scale as the morning grade)

Deliberately scored out of 8 so the pre-open call and the realized day can be compared directly, day after day. A "Selective" call on a day that ran 1.8× ATR is a miss, and it should read as one.

ComponentMeasuresScoring
Range (0–3) The day's high-to-low range as a multiple of ATR(14). ≥1.25× → 3 · ≥1.0× → 2 · ≥0.75× → 1 · below → 0
Direction (0–2) Where the close landed inside the day's range — 100% is on the high, 0% on the low. Trend day → 2 · close in the outer third either side → 1 · closed mid-range → 0
Follow-through (0–3) Whether the published levels were actually reached. One point each: opening range broken · ORB first target hit · gap filled

Trend day means the session both expanded (≥1× ATR) and closed in the top or bottom quarter of its range — the profile that actually pays. Profile summarises range alone: ≥1.25× ATR is Expansion, ≥0.75× is Normal, below that is Compression. Grades use the same thresholds as the morning: 7–8 A+ · 5–6 A · 3–4 B · 0–2 C. The card reports the gap against the pre-open call, and treats a difference of 2 points or more as a genuine miss in either direction.

Pattern outcomes

SetupResolved against
ORBDid the day's high or low exceed the opening range, and did it travel a further 1.5× ATR to T1?
Gap FillDid price trade back through the prior close at any point in the session?
Gap ContinuationDid the session extreme in the gap's direction reach T1 or T2? A fill back to the prior close instead means the thesis broke.

Prior session setups

Outside Day and Engulfing are next-session setups: they are published one evening and executed the following day. This section closes that loop — it takes the setup that fired on the previous bar and resolves its entry, stop and targets against the session that just finished.

ResultMeaning
Not triggeredPrice never reached the entry. No trade.
StoppedTriggered, then hit the stop.
T1 / T2 hitTriggered and reached the first or second target.
Triggered, openEntered, but neither stop nor target was reached within the session.

Stops and targets are resolved against the session's high and low, so both may register as reached within a single day. Order of events inside the session is not reconstructed.
